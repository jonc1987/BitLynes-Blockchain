How BitLynes works

A Lyne is a signed transfer of LYN from one BitLynes address to another. Pending Lynes are collected into blocks, and miners receive LYN for adding blocks to the chain.

This is a prototype currency. “Add Funds” creates test LYN directly on-chain.
