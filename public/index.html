Miner Network

Choose who
moves the Lyne.

Regular wallets do not mine. Miner groups independently confirm the Lynes assigned to them and earn their published fee.

Create a miner group

Set the fee senders pay when they choose your group. AutoMiner is the built-in automatic option at 0.002%.

How miner selection works

1. Sender chooses
Every new Lyne is assigned to one miner group.
2. Fee is signed
The miner choice and exact fee become part of the signed Lyne.
3. That miner confirms
Other miner groups cannot scoop up that Lyne.
